## Cyril Gervais, tourism data analyst

**Data analyst specializing in tourism, with over 10 years of experience in the industry: I turn your tourism data into clear, actionable insights to optimize your actions and your destination strategy.**

> I help **tourist offices**, **regional and departmental tourism boards**, **local authorities** and **tourism businesses** set up their tools and grow their data and AI practices.

## What I offer:

- **Data collection**: open data (DATAtourisme, data.gouv.fr...), web scraping, web analytics, CRM, APIs
- **Processing and storage**: accessible, well-structured databases (Postgres, DuckDB, no-code...)
- **Data analysis**: visitor numbers, occupancy rates, supply and customer mix, trends
- **Data visualization**: interactive dashboards, data visualizations and maps, for print or web
- **AI and automation**: RAG, vector databases, automated data pipelines
